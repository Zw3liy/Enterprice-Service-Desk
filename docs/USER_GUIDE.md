# End-User & Technician Guide

## Sign in
Open the site URL and sign in with your organization account (password or SSO at `/identity/sso/`).

## Customer portal (`/portal/`)
- Submit service requests from the catalog
- Track your tickets and add public comments
- Search the help center

## Technician workspace
- **Dashboard** — open volume, breaches, recent tickets
- **Tickets** — queue, filters, create/update, assign, work logs, attachments
- **Incidents / Problems / Changes** — ITIL processes
- **CMDB / Assets** — configuration items and hardware records
- **Knowledge** — publish and search articles
- **Field service** — work orders for on-site work (API/mobile)

## AI assistant (`/ai/assistant/`)
Ask natural-language questions; the assistant classifies intent and suggests knowledge articles. Optional LLM providers via environment keys.

## Notifications
Bell icon lists in-app notifications. Email is sent when configured.

## MFA
Users with privileged access should enroll an authenticator at `/mfa/setup/` and store backup codes safely.
